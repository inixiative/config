Chapter 1

Cooperation needs structure, not goodwill

Long-form chapters read on paper: the light theme, a 68-character measure and a slower leading.

Groups fail to cooperate less often from bad intent than from missing structure: nobody can see who holds what, or who may hand it on. A grant you can revoke changes that, because the holder's reach is written down and checked.

What a grant carries

Every grant names its grantor, its holder and the narrowing between them. Revising it applies at once.

  • who granted it, and from what they could do themselves
  • what it reaches, as a lens over the data
  • when it ends
You can only hand on what you could do yourself.
